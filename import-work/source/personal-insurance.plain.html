How can I access my digital proof of insurance?
You can access your digital proof of insurance in the “Documents” section of the app. For secure access, add the widget to your iPhone or Android device. If you have an iPhone, you can also use the Wallet app.
How can I get an insurance quote with Intact?
You have two options! First, you can click on “Get a quote” located at the top right of your screen. You can also find it all through the website. Second, you can also give us a call and get your insurance quote from an insurance representative.
How can I view the status of a claim?
You can view the status of your claim by accessing your Client Centre or the Intact insurance mobile app. You can also add photos, invoices or relevant documents to your claim to facilitate the review process.
How can I save on my car or house insurance?

There are many ways to save on car and home insurance:

  • Combine your car and home insurance and take advantage of the exclusive benefits.
  • Insure more than one vehicle for the same address.
  • Sign up to the my Drive™ program.
  • Choose a higher deductible to lower your premium.
  • Implement preventative measures in your home, like an alarm system.
  • Research some coverage options that might allow flexibility. Your insurance representative can help you determine precisely the level of coverage you need.
Do you offer insurance for new drivers?
It’s always a pleasure to take care of new drivers. As you are still a novice behind the wheel, your premium may be higher than someone with several years of experience. However, if you are eligible to enroll in my Drive™, you could save on your insurance based on your driving habits with the program. Please reach out to an insurance representative to find out more about available protections and discounts.
